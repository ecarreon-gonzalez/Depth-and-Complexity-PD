Dallas ISD Mathematics Curriculum & Instruction

  • Four-Finger Scale: Real-time icon classification practice
  • Turn & Talk: Reflection on model lesson opportunities
  • Make Your Mark: Targeted exploration of Carnegie HQIM tasks
  • 4 Corners: Implementation reflection & partner matchmaking
